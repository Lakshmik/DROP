 #4 - Callable Bond Pricer
 --------------------------
 --------------------------
  4.1 - Bonds covered - Fixed, Floating, and Flipper (fixed-to-float and float-to-fixed)
  4.2 - Tree Construction
	- Mean-reverting Gaussian Diffusion Trinomial Tree ∆X_t=[θ_t-aX_t ]∆t+σ_t ∆W_t
	- Deterministic Drift Function g - ∆g_t=[θ_t-ag_t ]∆t
		- x_t=X_t-g_t
		- ∆x_t=-ax_t ∆t+σ_t ∆W_t
		- Initial x is 0
	- Discretizing the Time Dimension - Initialized with exercise and coupon pay dates
	- American Exercise:
		- First array of exercise dates generated between exercise start and end with the minimal exercise gap
		- Exercise Gap changes over time
		- Long-dated options => increasing the density of exercises has a small valuation impact while requiring more computational resources
		- Time Gap must be greater than Tree Step Length
	- x Nodes: ∆x_i=σt_i √(3(t_i-t_(i-1) ) )
  4.3 Construction of Time Line
	- Branching Process: Approximating ∆x_t=-ax_t ∆t+σ_t ∆W_t as closely as possible
	- Node mean x: M_(i,j)=E[x(t_(i+1) )-x(t_i )|x_(i,j) ]=x_(i,j) [e^(-a(t_(i+1)-t_i ) )-1]
	- Node Variance: V_i=V[x(t_(i+1) )-x(t_i )|x_(i,j) ]=(σ^2 (t_i ))/2a [1-e^(-a(t_(i+1)-t_i ) ) ]
		- α=(x_(i,j)+M_(i,j)-x_(i+1,k))/(Δx_(i+1) )
		- p_u=(V_i^2)/(2∆x_(i+1)^2 )+(α^2+α)/2
		- p_m=1-(V_i^2)/(∆x_(i+1)^2 )-α^2
		- p_u=(V_i^2)/(2∆x_(i+1)^2 )+(α^2-α)/2
	- Central of the three nodes time t_(i+1) is chosen as close as possible by setting k to k=Round ((x_(i,j)-M_(i,j))/(∆x_(i+1) ))
  4.4 Standard Roll-back
	- df (i,j,t)=df_proj (i,j,t)∙e^(-∫_(t_i)^t▒spread(s)ds)
	- step (i,j)=∑_l▒p(i+1,l| i,j) ∙V(i,j).S_p (i,j)+[1-S_p (i,j)]∙R
	- v(i-1,j,k)=df(i-1,j,k)∙step(i-1,j)
	- Full Coupon PV at node: additional_cpn (i-1,j)=∑_l▒〖Notional_l×Coupon_l×DCF_l×df(i-1,CouponPayDate_l ) 〗
	- Accrual Coupon PV: accres(i-1,j)=Notional_l×Coupon_l×[DCF_(l,Full)×df(i-1,j,CouponPayDate_l )-DCF_(l,Partial) ]
	- Notification Period:
		- ValueToExercise=(v(i-1,j,k)+accrued_Adjusted (i-1,j)-fee_(i-1))/df(i-1,j,NotificationDate_k )  where
		- accrued_Adjusted (i-1,j)=Notional_l×Coupon_l×[DCF_(l,Full)×df(i-1,j,CouponPayDate_l )/df(i-1,j,NotificationDate_k ) -DCF_(l,Partial) ]

 #5 - Refundings and Redemption Provisions
 --------------------------
 --------------------------
  5.1 - What is a Refunding
	- Debt retired/refinanced using new issue whose proceeds pay principal, interest, and redemption premium
	- Bonds to be refunded are "prior" or "refunded"; New bonds issued are referred to as "refunding" bonds
	- Sequence in the Refunding Process
		- Issuer deposits the proceeds of the refunding bonds in an escrow fund, typically with a trustee
		- Invest the proceeds in permitted investments
	- Calculation of PV Savings:
		- PV of the prior bonds debt service before refunding against the PV of the debt service after the refunding
  5.2 - Types of Refundings
	- Current Refunding - Proceeds of refunding applied to principal/interest/premium on refunded within 90 days of the refunding issue
	- Advance Refunding - Proceeds of refunding applied to principal/interest/premium on refunded after 90 days of the refunding issue
	- High-to-low Refunding - Refunding in a lower rate environment
	- Low-to-high Refunding - Refunding in a higher rate environment
	- Forward Refunding - Purchase agreement between an issuer/under-writer for a future refunding
	- Cross-over Refunding - Proceeds of refunding applied to pay interest on refunding bonds until the prior bonds mature/called
  5.3 - Defeasance
	- Defeasance agreement - Applicable redemption and maturity dates of bonds to be refunded
		- Other requirements such as the types of escrow investment permitted
		- Issuer may exchange escrowed securities prior to the maturity or to call the escrowed-to-maturity bonds prior to maturity
	- Legal Defeasance - Lien released, so no outstanding debt
	- Economic Defeasance - Lien not released, so yes outstanding debt
  5.4 - Escrow Fund and Investments
	- SLGS are sold by the Treasury Department to states, municipalities, and other local governments
	- Permitted investments other than SLGS may be purchased on the open market
	- Tax code restricts yield earned on the escrow
		- Proceeds are invested in those vehicles that will result in the highest yield available and permitted
		- SLGS are obtained with yields that comply with the tax code’s yield requirements and closely match the yield on the refunding bonds
		- Negative arbitrage on the escrow  => yield on escrow investments less than the yield permitted by the tax code
	- Defeasance Defrayment Types:
		- Net funding defeasance => Escrow fund + investment earnings is calculated sufficient for debt servicing/redemption premium
		- Gross/Full Cash defeasance => Escrow fund alone is calculated sufficient for debt servicing/redemption premium
		- Sufficiency calculated to one or more redemption dates, or to maturity
		- Escrowed-to-maturity => Sufficiency to maturity
	- Yield Limitations and Rebate
		- In certain accounts under the indenture, the earnings may not exceed the bond yield.
		- In other cases earnings may exceed the bond yield but are subject to rebate.
			- Issuer must pay corresponding tax
		- Yield limitations may also affect proceeds of prior bonds that have not been spent at the time of refunding
			- Tax code treats these transferred proceeds as as refunding proceeds paid over time
			- Transferred proceeds penalty => Rebate amount/payments in lieu of rebates - due to transfer

 #9 - PnL Explain
 --------------------------
 --------------------------
	- Attribution Steps – EOD Revaluation: PnL(T+1B)=V_EOD (T+1B)-V_EOD (T)
	- PnL Attribution Steps:
		– PnL(T+1B)=PnL_Explained (T+1B)+PnL_Unexplained (T+1B)
		- PnL_Explained=PL_θ+PL_SOD+PL_∆+PL_Π+PL_fix
	- Model Based Valuations:
		- Dynamic Nature of Calibration Instruments
			- Price of the traded calibration deals fluctuate
			- Calibration trades themselves expire and can be replaced
		- Contribution from Trade Activity: Position Π(t) => new trade activity, up-sizing
		- Contribution from Fixings and Results: Fix(t)
		- Full Revaluation at EOD:
			- V_EOD (T)=V(T,m(T,s(T)),Fix(T),Π(T))
			- V_EOD (T+1B)=V(T+1B,m(T+1B,s(T+1B)),Fix(T+1B),Π(T+1B))
	- PnL Attribution Steps – Time Decay - V_θ (T+1B) and 〖PnL〗_θ
		- Forward Roll of Curves to T+1B: Moving the date forward from T→T+1B
	- Risk-Based Valuations V ̃_PRED (T+1B) and PnL_∆
		- PnL_∆=∑_i▒〖∂V/(∂s_i ) ∆s_i 〗+1/2 ∑_ij▒〖(∂^2 V)/(∂s_i ∂s_j ) ∆s_i ∆s_j 〗
		- V ̃_PRED (T+1B)=V_SOD (T+1B)+PnL_∆
		- Changes to the Trading Portfolio: Π(T+1B)=Π(T)+Π_NEW (T+1B)
		- EOD Valuation V_(EOD-Only) (T+1B):
		- Existing portfolio Π(T): new EOD fixings/portfolio elements
			- Against the new market state data s(T+1B)
		- EOD Valuation V_(EOD-Π) (T+1B) and PnL_Π: New trades, unwinds, up-sizing, etc.
		- EOD Valuation V_EOD (T+1B) and PnL_Fix
	- Theta Breakdown:
		- Total Rolled Metric - 〖TRV〗_(t+1B,m)=〖PV〗_(t+1B,m)+c_(t,t+1B)
		- θ(t,t+1B)=〖PV〗_(t+1B,SOD)-〖PV〗_(t,EOD)+c_(t,t+1B)=〖TRV〗_(t+1B,SOD)-〖PV〗_(t,EOD)
		-〖PV〗_(t,m)=c_t+〖NPV〗_(t,m)=c_t+∑_(t=1)^n▒〖P_m (t,s_i ) c_(s_i ) 〗
		- θ(t,t+1B)=
			∑_(i=1)^n▒〖P_SOD (t+1B,s_i ) c_(s_i ) 〗-[c_t+∑_(i=1)^n▒〖P_EOD (t,s_i ) c_(s_i ) 〗]+c_(t,t+1B)=(c_(t,t+1B)-c_t )+∑_(i=1)^n▒〖c_(s_i ) [P_SOD (t+1B,s_i )-P_EOD (t,s_i )] 〗=c_t  (P_EOD (t,t)-P_EOD (t,t+1B))/(P_EOD (t,t+1B) )+∑_(i=1)^n▒〖c_(s_i ) [P_SOD (t+1B,s_i )-P_EOD (t,s_i )] 〗
		- θ(t,t+1B)=θ_DECAY (t,t+1B)-θ_INSTRUMENT (t,t+1B)
			- θ_DECAY (t,t+1B)=〖TRV〗_(t+1B,ROLLED)-〖PV〗_(t,EOD)
			- θ_INSTRUMENT (t,t+1B)=〖TRV〗_(t+1B,SOD)-〖TRV〗_(t+1B,ROLLED)=〖PV〗_(t+1B,SOD)-〖PV〗_(t+1B,ROLLED)
		- Rolled Model Not Viable: Some SOD methods do not provide a viable model
			- m=Rolled(t+1B) => m=SOD(t+1B)
			- θ_DECAY (t,t+1B)
				=〖TRV〗_(t+1B,ROLLED)-〖PV〗_(t,EOD)=〖TRV〗_(t+1B,SOD)-〖PV〗_(t,EOD)
				=〖PV〗_(t+1B,SOD)+c_t-〖PV〗_(t,EOD)=〖PV〗_(t+1B,SOD)-(〖PV〗_(t,EOD)-c_t )
				=〖PV〗_(t+1B,SOD)-〖NPV〗_(tB,SOD)
			- θ_INSTRUMENT (t,t+1B)
				=〖PV〗_(t+1B,SOD)-〖PV〗_(t+1B,ROLLED)
				=〖PV〗_(t+1B,SOD)-〖PV〗_(t+1B,SOD)=0

 #10 - The SABR Model
 --------------------------
 --------------------------
	- Introduction
		- β=0 => Normal
		- β=1 => Log-normal
	- SABR Model Closed Form => Black '76 implied volatility
		- σ_Model (K,f_0 )=A.(z/χ(z) ).B
		- A=σ_0/((f_0 K)^((1-β)/2) [1+{(1-β)^2/24 〖log〗^2  f_0/K+(1-β)^4/1920 〖log〗^4  f_0/K+⋯}] )
		- B=[1+{(1-β)^2/24-〖σ_0〗^2/(f_0 K)^(1-β) +(ρβvσ_0)/(4(f_0 K)^((1-β)/2) )+〖2-3ρ〗^2/24 v^2 }+⋯]
		- z=v/σ_0  (f_0 K)^((1-β)/2)  log⁡〖f_0/K〗
		- χ(z)=log⁡〖(√(z^2-2ρz+1)+z-ρ)/(1-ρ)〗
	- Parameter Estimation
		- Implied Volatility Curve Shape
			- Parameter	"Curve Property"	Definition
			  --------- ----------------    ----------
			- σ_0		Level				The curve shifts upwards as it increases
			- β			Slope				The curve steepens as it decreases
			- ρ			Slope				The curve steepens as it decreases
			- v			Curvature			The curvature increases as it increases
		- Calibrate Parameters
			- Estimate β
			- Imply σ_0 from ρ and v
			- Calibrate ρ and v
			- A common industry practice: β=0.5
		- Step 1 - Estimate β
			- σ_(ATM,Market)≈σ_Model (f_0,f_0 )=σ_0/〖f_0〗^(1-β)  {1+[(1-β)^2/24  〖σ_0〗^2/〖f_0〗^(2-2β) +(ρβvσ_0)/(4〖f_0〗^(1-β) )+(2-3ρ^2)/24 v^2 ]T}
			- log⁡〖σ_(ATM,Market) 〗=log⁡〖σ_0 〗-(1-β)  log⁡〖f_0 〗
			- β be estimated from a linear regression between the log ATM volatilities and the log forward rate time series
		- Step 2 - Imply σ_0 from ρ and v
			- Invert the above equation to obtain the following cubic equation in σ_0
			- [((1-β)^2 T)/(24〖f_0〗^(2-2β) )] 〖σ_0〗^3+[ρβvT/(4〖f_0〗^(1-β) )] 〖σ_0〗^2+[1+(2-3ρ^2)/24 v^2 T] σ_0-σ_(ATM,Market) 〖f_0〗^(1-β)=0
		- Smallest Positive Solution σ_0
			- σ_0, as the smallest positive real root to this equation.
			- Function of the parameters ρ and v, which will be calibrated in the next step
		- Step 3 - Calibrate ρ and v:
			- Choose parameters that bring the model volatilities down to the market quote implied volatilities
				- (ρ,v)=■(arg⁡min@ρ,v) ∑_i▒[σ ̂_i (ρ,v)-σ_(i,Market) ]^2 
		- Step 3 Full Surface Calibration
			- Steps 1, 2, and 3, SABR in its primitive form is relatively straightforward to calibrate
			- Calibration to a volatility surface σ(K,T) (or a volatility cube σ(K,T_α,T_β ) in the case of swap options), process complicated
			- So SABR fixes the forward rate (and T) and calibrates to a smile/skew curve with respect to the K

 #11 - The SABR Volatility Model
 --------------------------
 --------------------------
	- Dynamics
		- Stochastic Dynamics of F(t) and A(t)
			- ∆F(t)=A(t) 〖F(t)〗^β ∆W(t)
			- ∆A(t)=αA(t)∆Z(t)
		- ∆W(t)∆Z(t)=ρ∆T
		- α≥0; 0≤β≤1
	- Asymptotic Solution
		- European Call Closed-form Solution - Asymptotic Solution using Tα^2 Series
	- Black Lognormal Model Implied Volatility
		- C(F)=F^β
		- F_Mid midpoint between F(T_0 ) and K – such as geometric average √(F(T_0 )∙K) or arithmetic average (F(T_0 )+K)/2
		- γ_1=1/C(F_Mid ) ∙dC(F_Mid )/(dF_Mid )=β/F_Mid 
		- γ_2=1/C(F_Mid ) ∙(d^2 C(F_Mid ))/(dF_Mid^2 )=-β(1-β)/(F_Mid^2 )
		- ς=α/σ(T_0 )  ∫_K^F(T_0 )▒dx/C(x) =α/σ(T_0 )(1-β)  [F(T_0 )^(1-β)-K^(1-β) ]
		- D(ς)=log⁡〖(√(1-2ρς+ς^2 )+ς-ρ)/(1-ρ)〗
		- σ_(IMPLIED,Lognormal)=α  log⁡(F(T_0 )/K)/D(ς)  {1+[(2γ_2-γ_1^2+1/(F_Mid^2 ))/24  (A(T_0 )C(F_Mid ) ) ̅^2/α+(ργ_1)/4  A(T_0 )C(F_Mid )/α+(2-3ρ^2)/24]ε}
		- K→F(T_0 ) => log⁡(F(T_0 )/K)/D(ς) => 1
		- σ_(IMPLIED,Normal)=α (F(T_0 )-K)/D(ς)  {[(2γ_2-γ_1^2)/24  (A(T_0 )C(F_Mid ) ) ̅^2/α+(ργ_1)/4  A(T_0 )C(F_Mid )/α+(2-3ρ^2)/24]ε}
	- SABR for the Negative Rates
		- ∆F(t)=σ(t) [F(t)+s]^β ∆W(t)
		- Shifts Included in Market Quotes
		- Anotonov, Konikov, and Spector (2015) Model
			- ∆F(t)=σ(t) |F(t)|^β ∆W(t)
			- ∆σ(t)=ασ(t)∆Z(t)
			- 0≤β≤1/2 and free boundary condition for F≡0
